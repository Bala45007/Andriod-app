Before you buy land, check these

Encumbrance certificate for the last 30 years or more. Patta and chitta in the seller's name. Survey number matches the sale deed. Guideline value for that street or survey number. Approval status of the layout (DTCP or CMDA) for house plots.

These are estimates for planning. Tax and duty rules change, and your case may have exemptions or surcharges not covered here. Confirm with the Sub-Registrar office or a chartered accountant before you act. The toolkit does not store or send anything you type.
